Our research focuses on radiation detection and monitoring, radiological engineering, health physics, and the integration of artificial intelligence and advanced modeling to address emerging challenges in nuclear science and engineering. The laboratory also actively collaborates across the advanced nuclear ecosystem to address emerging challenges, translate research into practical solutions, and support the development of safe, secure, resilient, and sustainable next-generation nuclear technologies while enhancing existing nuclear systems and infrastructure.
