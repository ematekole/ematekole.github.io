## Course Overview

In completing this course, the student be able to utilize the foundational concepts of radiation dosimetry reinforced in this course with prescribed methods to conduct dose assessment in occupational and medical radiation exposure scenarios.  

## Prerequisites

- MP 6756 (Radiation Physics) and MP 6757 (Radiation Detection).

## Textbooks

- Introduction to Radiological Physics and Radiation Dosimetry, Attix, Wiley, 1986/2004.
