## Course Overview

Student should be able to:
- Understand the fundamental concepts of internal exposures and dosimetry
- Understand the concept of dose coefficients pertaining to internal dosimetry
- Evaluate committed effective and equivalent dose
- Apply fundamental internal dose assessment methodologies for practical radiological monitoring and nuclear medicine problems


## Prerequisites

- Radiation interaction with matter
- System of radiation protection
- Radioactive decay
- Radiation measurements
