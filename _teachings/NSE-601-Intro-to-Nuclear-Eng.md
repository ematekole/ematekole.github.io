## Course Overview

An introduction to atomic and nuclear structure, radioactive decay, neutron interactions, reactor physics, steady-state and time-dependent reactor behavior, diffusion theory, radiation shielding requirements, reactor types, operational principles, and the regulatory framework for reactor licensing and safety.
**Credit hours: 3** required for NSE
**Intended audience:** First-year graduate students in nuclear science and engineering.
