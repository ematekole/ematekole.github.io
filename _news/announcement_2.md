Congratulations! I am pleased to inform you that you have been selected to receive a SIT Travel Award to attend the 2026 72nd Annual Radiation Research Society Meeting to be held at the Hilton Waikoloa Village, over the dates of October 4-8, 2026. A total of 160 SIT abstracts were submitted for consideration. These were judged, blindly, by 35 referees representing the radiation research community. We are extremely proud of the overall quality of the abstracts submitted for this meeting and are particularly excited about those that were selected for travel award support.
